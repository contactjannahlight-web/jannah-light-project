✨ Welcome to Jannah Light ✨

We are an ambitious Algerian industrial project specialized in manufacturing high-quality LED bulbs, using modern technology and international standards. We aim to illuminate your homes and cities with the best energy-saving lighting solutions, with a pure Algerian touch.

We believe that quality starts with the details, which is why we use the latest machines, reliable raw materials, and a trained team to deliver a product that exceeds your expectations. Jannah Light – future-quality lighting, locally made.

🔧 LED Bulb Components – High Quality Manufacturing

Screw Base

E27/E14 brass base, conducts electricity and secures the bulb.

Heat Sink

Aluminum heat sink, dissipates heat to protect electronic chips.

LED Driver

Converts 220V AC to DC suitable for LED, with surge protection.

LED Chips

High-efficiency SMD chips LM-80 certified, bright light and low power consumption.

Diffuser Cover

Clear or frosted polycarbonate, spreads light evenly and protects internal components.
